For You 💌

Tap the cards baby. Every single one hides a reason why my heart slowly became yours without me even realizing it.

Tap to reveal why your specs destroy my heart emotionally.

The way you adjust your specs while talking seriously... God. That tiny habit is unfairly attractive. You don't even realize how adorable you look during those moments.

🌙 Card #2

Tap to reveal sleepy-you secrets.

Your sleepy texts feel softer. More honest. More vulnerable. And somehow that version of you makes me want to protect you forever.

🤍 Card #3

Tap to reveal what your closeness does to me.

The way you deliberately brush against me and then pretend you did nothing... Baby... those tiny touches stay in my chest for hours.

😂 Card #4

Tap to reveal your dangerous talent.

You somehow turn normal conversations into double meanings within seconds. And honestly? Watching you smirk after that is one of my favorite things ever.

💕 Card #5

Tap to reveal my favorite sound.

Your laugh. Especially the uncontrollable one. The one where you completely forget the world for a second. Happiness looks beautiful on you baby.

🫀 Card #6

Tap to reveal what healed me.

You made love feel safe instead of scary. And I don't think you realize how deeply that changed me emotionally.
You know baby... This gift was never actually about cute cards or romantic lines. It exists because I realized something after reading everything again properly. You were not hurt because of projects or comparisons alone. You were hurt because when you finally opened your heart completely... you felt alone in that moment. And that thought genuinely destroyed me emotionally.

I understood that when you finally gathered courage to explain every insecurity, every overthinking, every fear properly... what you needed most from me was presence. Not perfect words. Not solutions. Just emotional presence. And instead, my silence made you feel like I stepped away when you needed me the most.

Baby I swear that was never my intention. When I woke up and read everything together, I genuinely froze emotionally. I knew how deeply hurt you were. I knew this was not normal overthinking for you. And I became scared that whatever I replied in that moment would sound fake, dismissive, or would hurt you even more. But now I understand something very clearly: silence also hurts. Especially when someone is already emotionally breaking inside.

And the thing that hurts me most is knowing you don't open up this deeply easily. You hold so much inside. So when you finally did... I should've protected that vulnerability more carefully. Because your feelings are never “too much” for me baby. Never. I was just emotionally overwhelmed and instead of communicating that properly... I froze.

I also need you to know this very honestly: I have never looked at you as someone lesser than me. Not professionally. Not emotionally. Not in life. I know how much strength it took for you to survive everything these past few years. I know the amount of pressure, comparisons, disappointment, self-doubt and exhaustion you quietly carried. And still... you kept moving. That strength deserves respect, not comparison.

I am not asking you to suddenly stop overthinking. Or suddenly stop feeling insecure. I just never want you to feel like you are alone while feeling those things. Because you are not. You never will be.

And baby... I don't want us to become people who stop sharing emotions because we are scared the other person won't respond perfectly. I don't want us to lose that safe little world we created together. That place where we could remove every fake layer and just become ourselves. Messy. Emotional. Attached. Real.

This gift exists because I wanted you to feel something very clearly: I notice you. I listen to you. I care about your feelings deeply. And even when I fail sometimes... my love for you never changes. Never reduces. Never disappears.

You are still my comfort. Still my favorite notification. Still the person my heart searches for first. And honestly baby... even after mistakes, misunderstandings, overthinking, pain... I still only want you. Always you. ❤️
